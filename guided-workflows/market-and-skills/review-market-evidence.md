# Review market evidence

## Steps

1. Confirm the sample size, filters, and freshness shown on the page.
2. Identify repeated skills and the openings that support each signal.
3. Distinguish strong evidence from small-sample noise or incomplete descriptions.
4. Compare the top signals with the user’s target roles and current profile.
5. Recommend at most three skills for deeper evidence review or backlog consideration.

## Done when

- The user understands which signals are credible, tentative, or irrelevant to their search.
