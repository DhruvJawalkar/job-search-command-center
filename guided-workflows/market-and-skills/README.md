# Market & skills workflows

| Order | Workflow | Outcome | Time | Prioritize when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Review market evidence](review-market-evidence.md) | Interpret demand signals from reviewed openings. | 5–8 min | Market signals are available or newly refreshed. |
| 2 | [Capture skill evidence](capture-skill-evidence.md) | Accept, correct, or reject proposed evidence. | 5–10 min | The human-validation queue is non-empty. |
| 3 | [Build a personal skill backlog](build-personal-skill-backlog.md) | Prioritize skills worth developing. | 8–12 min | The user wants a focused learning plan. |
| 4 | [Turn a skill gap into preparation](turn-skill-gap-into-preparation.md) | Create a concrete preparation direction from one gap. | 5–8 min | A high-priority gap has been chosen. |

Prefer evidence from reviewed target roles over generic trend claims.
