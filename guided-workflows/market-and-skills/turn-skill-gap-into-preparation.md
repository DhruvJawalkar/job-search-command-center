# Turn a skill gap into preparation

## Steps

1. Select one high-priority backlog skill and review the evidence behind it.
2. Define the gap in observable terms, not as a broad topic to “learn.”
3. Propose a two-week outcome, such as a design walkthrough, implementation, mock interview, or written explanation.
4. Choose a small set of evidence artifacts and a realistic effort level.
5. Navigate to Preparation and offer to create or extend the matching track.

## Done when

- One skill gap has a clear preparation outcome, evidence definition, and next workspace.
