# Preparation workflows

| Order | Workflow | Outcome | Time | Prioritize when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Create a preparation track](create-a-preparation-track.md) | Define one outcome-focused practice track. | 5–8 min | No suitable track exists. |
| 2 | [Plan a two-week sprint](plan-a-two-week-sprint.md) | Commit a small set of achievable preparation tasks. | 8–12 min | Backlog exists and no sprint is active. |
| 3 | [Record a practice session](record-a-practice-session.md) | Preserve duration, result, confidence, and next step. | 3–5 min | Practice just finished or evidence is missing. |
| 4 | [Review sprint progress](review-sprint-progress.md) | Re-plan blocked or incomplete preparation work. | 5–10 min | A sprint is active or nearing completion. |

Keep preparation outcomes small enough to demonstrate with evidence.
