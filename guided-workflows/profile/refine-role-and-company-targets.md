# Refine role and company targets

## Steps

1. Review recent good and poor-fit openings for concrete patterns.
2. Separate must-haves, preferences, and open questions across level, scope, location, company type, and size.
3. Capture long-term career direction and culture values in language useful for comparing roles.
4. Draft focused field updates without deleting useful existing context.
5. Show the before-and-after meaning, then let the user save the changes.

## Done when

- The profile explains what a high-fit opportunity means beyond title keywords.
