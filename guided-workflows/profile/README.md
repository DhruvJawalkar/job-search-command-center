# Profile workflows

| Order | Workflow | Outcome | Time | Prioritize when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Configure search preferences](configure-search-preferences.md) | Save a useful baseline for role discovery. | 5–8 min | The profile is empty or incomplete. |
| 2 | [Refine role and company targets](refine-role-and-company-targets.md) | Clarify scope, level, company type, and career direction. | 8–12 min | Results are too broad or poorly matched. |
| 3 | [Manage technology preferences](manage-technology-preferences.md) | Tune emphasized and excluded technologies. | 5–8 min | Results repeatedly include unwanted technical work. |
| 4 | [Review the local data boundary](review-local-data-boundary.md) | Understand where personal data is stored and exposed. | 3–5 min | Privacy, backups, or workspace location is in question. |

Profile changes are local. Ask before replacing meaningful existing preferences.
