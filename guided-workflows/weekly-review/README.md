# Weekly review workflows

| Order | Workflow | Outcome | Time | Prioritize when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Review live weekly metrics](review-live-weekly-metrics.md) | Explain funnel health and select adjustments. | 5–8 min | The user has not frozen the week yet. |
| 2 | [Create a weekly snapshot](create-weekly-snapshot.md) | Preserve the current week’s evidence. | 3–5 min | Metrics are reviewed and ready to freeze. |
| 3 | [Add a reflection revision](add-reflection-revision.md) | Record what worked and what changes next. | 5–10 min | A snapshot exists or reflection needs refinement. |

Make clear that snapshots and reflection revisions are append-only.
