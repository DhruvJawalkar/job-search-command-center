# Choose today’s priorities

## Steps

1. Review due application actions, outreach follow-ups, imported priorities, and today’s preparation focus.
2. Rank items by deadline, opportunity value, dependency, and effort.
3. Propose three actions with a clear outcome and a realistic order.
4. If the sources or ordering should recur, open **Customize Summary** and configure the three positions, labels, and time windows with the user.
5. Review Today’s specialization and the configured schedule only when they affect the chosen outcomes; neither is mandatory.
6. Guide the first chosen action or navigate to its page.

## Done when

- The user has three agreed priorities, knows which one to start, and has saved recurring preferences when requested.

## Boundaries

- Drafts may be prepared, but do not send messages or submit applications.
