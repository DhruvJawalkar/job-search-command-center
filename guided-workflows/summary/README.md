# Summary workflows

| Order | Workflow | Outcome | Time | Prioritize when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Choose today’s priorities](choose-todays-priorities.md) | Turn visible signals into three concrete actions. | 5 min | There are due actions, active applications, or competing work. |
| 2 | [Understand the command center](understand-command-center.md) | Explain what each summary area means. | 3–5 min | The user is new or asks what they are seeing. |
| 3 | [Review application follow-ups](review-application-follow-ups.md) | Triage the most urgent application actions. | 5–10 min | Follow-ups are due or overdue. |
| 4 | [Choose the next workspace](choose-next-workspace.md) | Navigate to the page with the highest-value next action. | 2–3 min | The summary is understood but the next move is unclear. |

For a general page-help request, offer the best three based on due work; otherwise use the displayed order.
