# Resolve inbox and duplicates

## Steps

1. Open the Inbox review and identify candidates needing review or duplicate resolution.
2. Verify company, role, location, source URL, and warnings for one candidate at a time.
3. For a duplicate, compare the evidence and choose link, merge, import separately, or reject.
4. State what the chosen resolution will change, then let the user confirm it.
5. Verify that the resulting opportunity is visible once review is complete.

## Done when

- Selected candidates are resolved without accidental duplicate opportunity records.
