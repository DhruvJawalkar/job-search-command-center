# Review and decide

## Steps

1. Select an opening and inspect its source, role summary, fit rationale, risks, and observation history.
2. Compare it with the local profile without treating a numeric score as the decision.
3. Identify the strongest fit evidence, largest uncertainty, and any disqualifier.
4. Recommend one action: continue reviewing, shortlist, seek a referral, prepare an application, or archive.
5. Ask the user to decide before changing status or opening another workflow.

## Done when

- The opening has an explicit, user-approved next action supported by visible evidence.
