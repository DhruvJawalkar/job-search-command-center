# Names and branding

“Job Search Command Center,” its logo, and its distinctive project branding identify the original project created by Dhruv Jawalkar. The software license does not grant permission to use those marks to imply that a modified version, service, package, or distribution is official, endorsed, maintained, or approved by Dhruv Jawalkar.

Non-commercial forks and modifications must:

- retain the `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files and preserve conspicuous creator attribution;
- clearly state that they are modified and identify the modifier;
- avoid presenting themselves as the official project or an official release; and
- use a distinct name and visual identity when confusion with the official project is reasonably likely.

Truthful references such as “based on Job Search Command Center by Dhruv Jawalkar” are permitted when they do not imply endorsement.

Questions about branding permission may be raised through the official repository owned by `DhruvJawalkar`.
