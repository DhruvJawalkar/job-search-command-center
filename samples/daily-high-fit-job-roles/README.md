# Sample daily high-fit files

These two files provide an import-ready example of the daily discovery handoff:

- `2026-09-08-high-fit-openings.xlsx` contains ten ranked, fictional job listings on the required `High-Fit Openings` sheet.
- `2026-09-08-top-three-actions.txt` contains three ordered actions whose company names link them to the matching openings during import.

All companies, roles, descriptions, scores, and activity are synthetic. Links use `example.com` and are not real job postings.

To try the import, copy both files into the `daily-high-fit-job-roles` folder inside your selected local workspace. In the application, open **Opportunities** and select **Sync daily files**. Keep the matching date prefix when creating or renaming a workbook and action-file pair.

The workbook also serves as the editable template. Preserve the sheet name and exact headers when replacing its sample rows with generated results.
